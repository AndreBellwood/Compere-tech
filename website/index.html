Multi-Vendor Comparative Audit

Ingests multi-vendor PDF, Word, and Excel tender submissions alongside consulting specifications. Automatically normalizes parameter aliases and detects split options (e.g. Carrier Premium vs Carrier Budget).

  • Side-by-side adjudication up to 5+ vendors
  • Automatic vendor email & clue discovery

Standalone Parameter Extraction

Need to audit a lone specification or single vendor quotation? Switch to Standalone Extraction mode. COMPèRE extracts equipment schedules, project names, and demarcation notes without comparative pass/fail scoring.

  • Auto-detection of spec vs quotation nature
  • Dedicated parameter schedule corporate PDFs

Real-Time Currency & Unit Normalization

Eliminate manual unit conversions and exchange rate fluctuations. Evaluates bids across global currencies (ZAR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED) via live daily spot rates while normalizing SI Metric, US Imperial, and Asian unit conventions.

  • Spot rate conversion with original value in brackets
  • Dual units: kW / TR, l/s / CFM, °C / °F

Audit-Proof Corporate PDF Reports
